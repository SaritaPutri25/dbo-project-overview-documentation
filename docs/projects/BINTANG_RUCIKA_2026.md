## Project Goal
We're updating the Toko App to show the new rules for the Bintang Rucika reward program in 2026. The rules changed from last year, so we need to update the app so store owners understand what's new.

---

## Who Can Join?
- Regular retail stores only (no special stores like offices or projects)
- Must be approved by at least 1 Rucika distributor
- New stores: get approval first, then can join

---

## How to Win?
- Buy Rp1.5M worth of Rucika products total
- **Important:** Must buy at least 1 time each from: Pipes, Fittings, and Glue (doesn't have to be a big order, just 1 order each)
- Only orders from the store app and salesman app count
- Orders must be finished (closed or partial status)
- The app only shows progress up to Rp1.5M (but you can see all your orders in order history)

---

## What Winners Get?
- Free Asia trip for 2 people
- They need to pay some tax on the prize
- Winners must update their ID info

---

## What the Developers are Building

### Quick Floating Button
A button that takes store owners right to the program dashboard

### Updated Dashboard
Shows how much they've bought, broken down by type (Pipes, Fittings, Glue)

### Check If You Can Join
The system checks automatically: Are you a retail store? Are you approved by a distributor?

### Winner Upload
Winners can upload their ID and other files through the app

### Program Info
Explains the rules, what you need to buy, and what winners get
